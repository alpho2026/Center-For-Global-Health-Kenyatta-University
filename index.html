01 About the Center

Who We Are

Based at Kenyatta University, the Center for Global Health is a hub for collaborative health research, practical clinical innovation, and workforce training.

We work closely with healthcare providers, policymakers, communities, and global partners to generate locally relevant evidence, responsibly deploy emerging health technologies, and strengthen health systems to improve maternal, newborn, child, and community health outcomes.

Our Focus

Addressing high-priority public health and clinical needs in low-resource settings.

Our Approach

Stakeholder-driven collaboration connecting academic rigor with frontline healthcare delivery.

Our Commitment

Ethical, equitable, and sustainable health solutions tailored for local and regional impact.

02 Core Pillars

Research, Innovation, Training & Capacity Building

Our work bridges priority health research, point-of-care innovation, and frontline clinical capacity building.

Pillar I

Areas of Research

Conducting multidisciplinary clinical, epidemiological, and implementation research across four priority areas:

  • Noncommunicable diseases
  • Maternal, newborn and child health
  • Infectious diseases
  • Artificial intelligence and digital health
Pillar II

Innovation

Evaluating, adapting, and scaling accessible medical technologies and AI-assisted diagnostic tools designed to support clinical decision-making at the point of care.

  • Point-of-care ultrasound (POCUS)
  • AI-assisted screening & diagnostics
  • Context-appropriate digital health solutions
Pillar III

Training & Capacity Building

Developing structured training modules, hands-on clinical workshops, and mentorship programs that equip healthcare workers and researchers with practical skills.

  • Ultrasound training & diagnostic capacity
  • Clinical & research mentorship
  • Health workforce strengthening
